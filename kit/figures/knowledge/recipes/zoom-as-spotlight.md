Worked case, a graduate profile: a solid band breaks into the individual seats that were inside it, each seat
starting stacked at its own cluster's point on the band; then eight of those
very seats swell 2.5× in place and take a leader line and a title while the
other hundred fade to grey. Nothing is added — the highlight IS the zoom.
`pose: spotlight(HEROES, 2.5)` with `dim: ['crowd']` is the whole beat.

Its `HEROES` table: **pick highlighted marks by hand, not by formula.** One per
group at the same index reads as eight slots in a diagram; scattered — different
rows, two from one group, a group with nobody — reads as eight people picked out
of a room, which is the claim.
